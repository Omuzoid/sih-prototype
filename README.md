# 🇮🇳 LM-DVS — Legal Metrology Digital Verification System

> **Verify. Certify. Protect Every Measurement.**

LM-DVS is a **Smart India Hackathon prototype** that digitizes the verification and certification lifecycle of weighing and measuring instruments.

It connects **Citizens, Traders, Field Inspectors, and Government Authorities** through a unified digital platform.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 Citizens

* 📷 QR-based instrument verification
* 🔐 Tamper/seal verification
* 📜 Digital certificate viewing
* ⚠️ Report measurement concerns & complaints

### 🏪 Traders

* Register weighing/measuring instruments
* 💰 Verification fee calculator
* 💳 Payment workflow
* 📅 Inspection slot booking
* 📜 Digital certificates & expiry tracking

### 📱 Inspectors

* 📋 Digital inspection workflow
* 📍 GPS/geofence verification
* ⚖️ MPE measurement calculation
* 🤖 OCR reading simulation
* ✍️ Digital inspection sign-off
* 📴 Offline inspection & sync

### 🏛️ Government / Admin

* 📊 National compliance dashboard
* 🗺️ GIS-based instrument monitoring
* 🚨 Risk & anomaly detection
* 📋 Master instrument registry
* ⏰ Expiry monitoring
* 🧾 Complaint & audit tracking

---

## 🔄 How It Works

```text
Register Instrument
        ↓
Pay Verification Fee
        ↓
Book Inspection
        ↓
GPS-Verified Field Inspection
        ↓
MPE & Physical Verification
        ↓
Digital Certificate
        ↓
Tamper-Evident QR
        ↓
Citizen Verification
```

## 🔐 Prototype Notice

This project is a **demonstration prototype**. Features such as cryptographic verification, GPS, OCR, payments, and some AI/risk functionality are simulated.

A production version would integrate secure backend infrastructure, real digital signatures, government authentication, payment gateways, real GPS/OCR, and centralized databases.
